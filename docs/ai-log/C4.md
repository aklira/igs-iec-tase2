# C4 — Device control, SBO (link)

## Prompt

Run task C4 of `IMPLEMENTATION.md`: the Select, Operate and Set Tag operations of the devices, with the CheckBackID, the timeout and the
select state, as IEC 60870-6-503 §7.2.6 gives them.

## Summary

- `crates/igs-iec-tase2-link/src/device.rs`: `Device` with its state (IDLE or ARMED), the operation in progress, its tag and the owner of the
  tag. Select, Operate (accepted, then `complete` with the outcome), Local Reset, Set Tag, Get Tag (`tag`), the timer (`advance`,
  `next_deadline`), and the actions signalled (`take_actions`: Timeout, LocalReset, Success, Failure).
- `crates/igs-iec-tase2-link/tests/device.rs`: 23 tests, one per transition and rule, including the timeout, a second select, the timer
  that stops during an operation, the local reset, the two inhibit tags, and the owner of a VCC-specific device.
- The bilateral table: `device_timeout` is added. The CheckBackID becomes the integer of 16 bits of its MMS type (D-063, a correction of B1).
- `ErrorCode::TemporarilyUnavailable` is added, mapped to DataAccessError 2 in the client and the server.
- Decisions: D-063 (the CheckBackID type), D-064 (the cycle, its refusals, and the choices where the standard is silent).

## Found on the way

- B1 had stored the CheckBackID as a string, without a decision. The MMS type is an integer of 16 bits (802 §6.2.2, confirmed on page 27 of the
  PDF). Corrected in its own commit.
- 802 §5.2 calls the CheckBackName "a symbolic description", which disagrees with its type. The typed value is used (D-063).
- 054 §7.2.6.3.1 names the Select permission in the Operate. Read as a copy error; the Operate checks its own permission (D-064).
- The rustdoc of C3 linked a private item: a warning from `cargo doc`, fixed in its own commit.

## Not done

- The MMS mapping of the device to its variables (`<Device_Name>_SBO`, `_TAG`, the Tag_Value type): task C7 (server) and C6 (client).
- The CheckBackID of the Operate is not checked (D-064): recorded for the matrix (C8).
- The events of the device (`<device>_time`, `_reset`, `_success`, `_fail`): the link signals the actions; the MMS event conditions are the
  server's (C7). Annex G of 503 (2002) gives the 2002 names; the current text of §7.2.6.4 names them the same way.
