# I1 — The third-party peer of the interop bench (build)

## Prompt

Prepare the interop bench with a licensed third-party TASE.2 implementation, as a separate process and a black box. The maintainer
authorised, for the build only, a minimal correction of the peer's sources (decision D-068).

## Summary

- `bench/third-party-peer/build.sh`: extracts the peer's archive outside the repository, builds its example programs in a container,
  and copies them to a directory outside the repository. Its paths come from environment variables, and its optional patch is applied in
  the container.
- Two build defects were corrected by a patch kept outside the repository (D-068): two conditional statements wrapped in braces.
  The debug option of the peer is left at its default. The example programs build.
- The programs run as separate processes. Nothing of the peer is in the repository.

## Not done

- The bench service, the configuration of the peer's objects, and the scenarios in both directions: they need the peer's
  documentation, and the decision of the maintainer on the scope of the reading (D-068 covers the build only).
