# M1 — Scalars and time

## Prompt

Run task M1 of `IMPLEMENTATION.md`: time types, floating point and strings, and their conversions to and from MMS `Data`.
Read IEC 60870-6-503 §8.2 and IEC 60870-6-802 §6. Done when the round trips pass, the hand-written vectors match, and the
range checks refuse what they must.

## Summary

- **Conversions**: `crates/igs-iec-tase2-model/src/mms.rs` (the trait `MmsValue` with `to_mms` and `from_mms`, its errors
  `ValueError`: kind, range, text, format). The generator emits the conversions of the scalars (integers, unsigned,
  booleans, visible strings, single precision reals), so they follow the data. The base types (`builtin.rs`) and the
  time types (`time.rs`) are by hand.
- **Time**: GMT seconds (`Integer32`), the time stamp with milliseconds (`TimeStampExtended`, 0 to 999 milliseconds, D-011),
  and the conversions to and from `SystemTime` (D-012): rounded down, before 1970 included, refused outside 32 bits.
- **Strings**: the visible characters (space to tilde, D-014) and the maximum length, checked by `value::check_visible`.
- **Reals**: the single precision form of the codec, exponent width 8 (D-013). The generator refuses any other format.
- **Decisions**: D-004 (tested here), D-011 to D-014.
- **Tests**: `tests/conversions.rs` (18 tests): round trips at the limits; vectors written from the encodings (21.5 is
  `08 41 AC 00 00`; 2000-01-01 is 946 684 800; the limits of 32 bits); range refusals; kind refusals; the real form; the
  string refusals; the time stamp refusals and the rounding before 1970.
- **Gate**: `porte=1` (fmt, clippy, test, deny, codegen --check), recorded with the commit.

## Found on the way

- A visible string checked only by its length accepted a tab: the check now also refuses characters outside the visible set.
- A first version of the milliseconds conversion rounded a time before 1970 up, not down; the vector of −0.5 s exposed it.
- The model now depends on the MMS codec (path dependency, rule 9): the `Data` type of the conversions is the codec's.

## Reading

IEC 60870-6-503 §8.2.2 (visible strings), §8.2.4 (time stamps), §8.2.5 (the time stamp with milliseconds), §8.2.6 (time
intervals). IEC 60870-6-802 §6 (the scalars of the data types), through the data files. No standard text is in the code
or the documents.
